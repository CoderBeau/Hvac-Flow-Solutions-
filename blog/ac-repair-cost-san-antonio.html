AC Repair Cost in San Antonio (2026)

Updated September 2026 · HVAC Flow Solutions

National industry cost guides put most AC repairs between $150 and $650, with small electrical parts like capacitors at the low end and major components like compressors or evaporator coils reaching $1,200 to $3,000. Every San Antonio company sets its own pricing, so treat these as ballpark figures for budgeting, not a price list any contractor should match. The smartest way to know what YOUR repair costs is to compare a few written quotes side by side. Here is the general landscape, what moves the numbers, and how to compare quotes the right way.

What makes the price move

Warranty status. If your system is under 10 years old, the part may be covered under the manufacturer warranty and you only pay labor. Always ask the tech to check the serial number before approving a repair.

Refrigerant type. Older systems running phased-out refrigerant cost far more to recharge, and a big leak on one of those units often tips the math toward replacement.

Timing. July breakdowns during a heat wave meet peak demand. After-hours and weekend calls usually carry a premium. If the house is livable, a next-morning appointment can save real money.

Service level and warranty. Two reputable companies can quote the same repair differently and both be fair. One may include a longer labor warranty, higher grade parts, same-day response, or a maintenance plan with the work. That is why comparing quotes matters. Not to catch anyone out, but to see what each company includes and pick the fit that is right for your home and budget.

Repair or replace?

A useful rule of thumb: multiply the repair cost by the age of the system. If the result is over about $5,000, replacement deserves a serious look. A $500 repair on a 6 year old unit ($3,000) is an easy yes. A $1,500 repair on a 14 year old unit ($21,000) is money into a machine at the end of its life, especially with San Antonio summers driving your electric bill through an old system's inefficiency. A good contractor will walk you through both numbers instead of pushing one.

How to compare repair quotes the right way

Get the diagnosis and price in writing before work starts, ask whether the trip fee is credited toward the repair, ask about the warranty check, and for anything over a few hundred dollars, compare quotes and what each one includes. Frequently asked questions

How much does an AC service call cost in San Antonio?

Most San Antonio companies charge a diagnostic or trip fee in the range of $75 to $150, and many apply it toward the repair if you hire them. Always ask whether the fee is credited before you book.

Is it worth repairing a 12 to 15 year old AC?

Use the 50% rule: if a repair costs more than half the price of a comparable new system, replacement is usually the better money. A $2,500 compressor on a 14 year old unit rarely makes sense; a $300 capacitor usually does. Age, refrigerant type, and summer electric bills all factor in.

Why is refrigerant so expensive?

You pay for the refrigerant itself plus the labor to find and fix the leak. Simply topping off a leaking system is throwing money away, because the new refrigerant leaks out too. A proper repair includes leak detection, the fix, and a recharge, which is why quotes vary so much.

How do I know a repair quote is fair?

Get the diagnosis and price in writing, ask whether the trip fee is credited toward the work, ask what warranty comes with the repair, and compare quotes from more than one company.
